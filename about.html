OUR STORY

A Vision for Industrial Reliability

At Power Equipment's System (P) Limited, we started with a vision to simplify multi-brand electrical procurement for the industrial sector. Over the years, we have integrated manufacturing capabilities in Bagroda to provide our partners in cement and railways with more than just products—we provide technical authority and dependable coordination.

Every panel we build is a testament to our commitment to excellence and industrial precision.

CAPABILITY

Technical Coordination & Multi-Brand Access

Power Equipment's System (P) Limited combines authorised channel-partner access, industrial product knowledge and panel-building capability to support dependable electrical infrastructure for industrial customers.

The company is positioned for customers who need timely supply, practical technical coordination and a single point of contact for multi-brand electrical requirements.

MANUFACTURING

Precision Manufacturing in Bagroda

Our Bagroda facility is a high-precision manufacturing hub dedicated to the technical execution of complex electrical systems. We bridge the gap between product selection and physical implementation, ensuring every panel meets the rigorous standards of cement and railway industries.

By maintaining an in-house panel-building capability, we provide a single point of contact for multi-brand requirements. Our technical team coordinates the precise assembly of VFD panels, LV switchgear, and control panels, delivering reliable infrastructure for industrial operations.
